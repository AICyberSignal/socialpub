HomeTerms of Service

SocialPub is a personal desktop application operated by [Your name]. It publishes our own videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: only what it needs to post, stored on our own computer, never sold or shared.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: the basic profile of the authorized account (open ID, username, display name and avatar, through the user.info.basic scope). It also reads the posting options TikTok returns for that account, such as the allowed privacy levels and maximum video length. It uses video.upload and video.publish only to upload and post videos that the account owner chose to publish.
  • YouTube and Instagram: the channel or account name, plus permission to upload videos and add them to playlists on that account.

The app doesn't read followers, messages, comments, contacts or any other user's data.

Where data is stored

The access and refresh tokens issued by each platform are stored only in a file on the operator's computer. They are sent only to the platform that issued them, through its official API. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Videos and their text are sent only to the platform they are being published to.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. To remove the app's access, revoke it in the platform's settings. For TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. Revoking access makes the stored tokens stop working, and deleting the app's token file removes them completely. You can also email us and we will delete them.

Children

The app isn't intended for children and doesn't knowingly process children's data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
