SocialPub

A personal desktop tool that publishes our own videos to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

What it does

SocialPub runs on our own computer. When a finished video is placed in its upload folder, it adds the prepared title, description and hashtags, and publishes the video at a scheduled time to our own channels through each platform's official API:

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API)
  • Instagram Reels (Instagram Graph API)
  • TikTok (TikTok Content Posting API)

We use it for our short explainer series about artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, AI & Cyber Signal and AI SHIFT, in English and Arabic.

Who can use it

SocialPub is not a public service. It is used only by its operator, to post content we created ourselves to accounts we own. It doesn't access, collect or post anything for other people's accounts.
